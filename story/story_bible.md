# THE LAST WATCH — Knights Reactor Story Bible
*Draft 1, for Luis to review. Grounded medieval: no magic, no monsters. The fight is with men, winter, hunger, and the war inside each knight.*

---

## The Premise (one sentence)
When a bought king disbands the Order of the Gate, five broken knights keep their oath anyway, holding a mountain pass through one long winter with no pay, no orders, and no one watching but God.

**Tagline:** *We hold the gate. Christ is King.*

**What every episode is really about:** the battle a man fights inside himself before he ever fights at the gate. Each episode is one knight in one moment of that battle. The lesson is the one in the script; the story is just where we film it.

---

## The World

### The Kingdom of Aldmere
A small Christian kingdom in the north, about 1190. The old king, a just man, is dead. His young son rules in name only. The real power belongs to **Lord Varric Hale**, a marcher lord who has bought the court with grain, silver and debt.

To the north, past the mountains, are the **Hollow Men**: raiding clans driven south by famine. They aren't monsters, just hungry, desperate, ruthless men. Every winter they test the pass.

### The Gate at Dunmarch
A stone gatehouse that seals the only road through the mountains. For two hundred years the **Order of the Gate** held it: a brotherhood of knights sworn to guard the pass and the villages below.

**The Oath**, spoken at every vigil:
> *"I hold the gate. I keep the watch. I fear no man, and I serve one King."*

At the start of the saga, Varric persuades the court that the Order is "too costly". The Order is disbanded, its pay stopped, its men told to go home. Most do. Five stay.

### Why it matters
If the gate falls, the Hollow Men pour into the valley in winter, and the first place they reach is **Hollow Ford**, the village where the knights' families live.

## Director's Look (for every scene)
- **Light:** real light only. Forge fire, candle, torch, overcast daylight, low winter sun, moonlight on snow. Never neon, never glowing magic.
- **Textures:** wet stone, rusted iron, wool, wax, mud, frost, horsehair, leather straps.
- **Color:** iron grey, bone white, deep brown, ember orange. Red is rare and means blood, wine or warning.
- **Faces:** the knights almost always wear their helms. We know them by their armor, their cape, and their one signature object. (This keeps every knight looking the same from video to video.)
- **Weather follows the season:** autumn rain → first snow → deep winter → thaw. No fog in every shot.
- **Avoid:** standing on hilltops, staring at horizons, empty battlefields, slow walks toward camera. Show a man **doing** something.

### Places we keep returning to
| Place | What happens there | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| **The Gatehouse** | The watch, the bell, the wall, the arrow slits | Cold stone, wind, torchlight |
| **The Forge** | Repairing armor and blades; confession through work | Fire, sparks, sweat |
| **The Chapel Ruin** | Prayer, vigils, the oath; a roof half fallen, one stained-glass window still whole | Candle, snow falling inside |
| **Hollow Ford** | Families, children, the mill, the river crossing | Warm windows, mud, smoke |
| **Varric's Hall at Greymoor** | Feasts, gold, wine, offers, comfort | Rich and warm, but rotten |
| **The Tithe Barn** | The Order's last grain and coin, counted by candle | Dust, ledger, rats |
| **The Winter Road** | Patrols, tracks in snow, ambushes | White silence |

### Recurring motifs
- **The Gate Bell:** rung once at dusk ("the watch is kept"), three times for danger.
- **"We hold."** The brothers' two-word answer to anything.
- **The Ledger:** the Order's book of names and debts. Who stayed and who left is written in it.
- **Bread broken in five:** the brothers share whatever they have.

## The Cast

### 1. THE VETERAN: Ser Aldric of Dunmarch
**Look:** dented steel plate armor, torn dark cape, closed scratched helm. **Signature object:** the Order's iron key to the gate, worn on a chain.
**Who he is:** the last Captain of the Order, in his fifties. He has held the gate for thirty winters.
**His wound:** ten years ago he ordered a retreat to save his men. A village burned because of it. He has never forgiven himself.
**His battle:** responsibility, leadership, carrying the weight alone, obedience when obedience costs.
**His arc:** from a man who carries everything alone → a leader who trusts his brothers and God with what he can't carry.
**He says:** *"A captain doesn't get to be tired. He gets to be faithful."*

### 2. THE WANDERER: Ser Rowan Ashby
**Look:** battered grey steel armor, heavy mud-stained cape, weathered closed helm. **Signature object:** one silver coin he can never bring himself to spend.
**Who he is:** left the Order years ago to sell his sword for money in the south. He comes back broke, ashamed, and too late to be welcomed.
**His wound:** he left his wife **Maren** and his son **Tobin** in Hollow Ford "for their own good" and sent home less every year.
**His battle:** money, debt, provision, chasing more, leading his family.
**His arc:** from chasing coin → a husband and father who stays, and a steward of little.
**He says:** *"I crossed three kingdoms for gold. Everything I needed was at the river crossing."*

### 3. THE BLACKENED: Ser Garrick Vane
**Look:** blackened steel plate, tattered cape in shreds, scarred closed helm. **Signature object:** a scorched gauntlet bound with rope.
**Who he is:** the strongest fighter in the Order, and the most dangerous to himself.
**His wound:** his armor turned black in the village fire from Aldric's retreat. Garrick lost his brother there. He blames Aldric, and he blames God.
**His battle:** anger, revenge, a temper that wants to swing first.
**His arc:** from rage → strength under control. The hardest hit he ever lands is the one he holds back.
**He says:** *"Anyone can burn. Try standing in the fire and not becoming it."*

### 4. THE PENITENT: Ser Edmund Clare
**Look:** ancient dulled steel plate, faded torn surcoat, heavy hooded cape, scratched helm. **Signature object:** a wooden rosary worn smooth, wrapped around his sword hilt.
**Who he is:** once the most admired knight in the Order. Then he broke his vow (a secret sin with a nobleman's wife) and was cast out in disgrace. He came back to the gate on foot, alone, and asked to serve as the lowest man.
**His wound:** shame. He thinks grace is for other men.
**His battle:** lust, temptation, secret sin, shame, purity.
**His arc:** from hiding in penance → walking in the light, forgiven. In the end he is the one who leads the brothers in prayer.
**He says:** *"The sin was in the dark. So I went back into the light, and kept walking."*

### 5. THE SOLITARY: Ser Tomas Wren
**Look:** tarnished steel armor, stained campaign cape, closed dented helm. **Signature object:** a small wooden horse carved for a child, tucked in his belt.
**Who he is:** the youngest of the five, and the one who never left. He keeps the night watch alone, every night.
**His wound:** his young wife died of fever last spring. The wooden horse was for the child they lost with her.
**His battle:** doubt, fatigue, loneliness, discipline when no one is watching, "where is God?"
**His arc:** from a man going through the motions → the quiet backbone of the Order, whose faith is proven in the dark.
**He says:** *"No one sees the night watch. That's why it matters."*

### Supporting cast
- **Father Edric:** the old priest who stayed at the chapel ruin. He feeds the knights and tells them the truth. The mentor.
- **Lord Varric Hale:** the antagonist. Never shouts; always offers. Gold, comfort, titles, "a reasonable life". He is temptation in a fur collar.
- **Maren Ashby:** Rowan's wife. Stronger than he deserves. Runs the mill and the village when the men are gone.
- **Tobin Ashby:** Rowan's son, about 10. Wears a wooden helmet and copies the knights. The future the knights are guarding.
- **Brann of the Hollow:** the raiders' war-chief. Not evil for evil's sake; he's feeding his own people. That makes him dangerous.

## The Saga

### Season 1: THE GATE (autumn → first snow)
*The Order is disbanded. Five stay. Varric starts buying them one by one. The first raid comes.*

| # | Episode | Knight | The moment we film | The battle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Decree** | Veteran | A royal messenger nails the order to the gate door; Aldric reads it, takes it down, and rings the bell at dusk anyway | Obedience to God over men |
| 2 | **Five Stayed** | All | The Ledger is opened; Aldric crosses out forty names. Five remain | Faithfulness |
| 3 | **The Coin** | Wanderer | Rowan stands at Maren's door, the silver coin in his fist, and can't knock | Shame, family, coming home |
| 4 | **The Offer** | Blackened | In Varric's warm hall, a bag of gold slides across the table toward Garrick's scorched gauntlet | Money and loyalty |

| 6 | **The Letter** | Penitent | A perfumed letter arrives from the nobleman's wife. Edmund holds it over the candle | Temptation, purity |
| 7 | **Empty Granary** | Wanderer | The Tithe Barn: Rowan counts the last coins by candle, then gives half to the village | Provision, generosity |
| 8 | **Swing First** | Blackened | Garrick catches a starving Hollow boy stealing bread and raises his sword, then lowers it | Anger, mercy |
| 9 | **The Captain's Weight** | Veteran | Aldric at the forge at 3 a.m., repairing every brother's armor before his own | Leadership, burden |
| 10 | **Tobin's Helmet** | Wanderer | Tobin, in his wooden helmet, copies Rowan's guard stance in the stable yard; Rowan corrects his grip | Fatherhood, example |

| 13 | **Three Bells** | All | The bell rings three times. Five knights arm in silence | Courage |
| 14 | **We Hold** | All | Kneeling in the chapel ruin, snow falling through the roof, the oath spoken together. Cut to black at the first blow on the gate | Faith before the battle |

### Season 2: THE WINTER SIEGE (deep winter)
The Hollow Men lay siege. Food runs out. Varric offers to send relief, at a price. Garrick and Aldric finally face the old fire. Rowan must choose between the gate and getting his family out. Edmund leads the brothers in prayer when Aldric falls wounded. Tomas holds the wall alone for one night that becomes legend.

### Season 3: THE CROWN (thaw → spring)
The siege breaks. The truth reaches the young king: Varric sold the pass. The five ride to the capital. A trial, a betrayal, and a choice between revenge and justice. The Order of the Gate is restored, and the Ledger fills again.

## Rules for Every Episode (so each 20-second video works on its own)
1. **One knight, one moment, one lesson.** Someone who has never seen another episode must still get it.
2. **Open on the struggle, not the scenery.** First shot: hands, an object, a choice about to be made.
3. **The plot is the backdrop, the lesson is the point.** The script still ends with one clear action for today.
4. **Scripture is woven in, not quoted.**
5. **Show it physically.** Anger is a raised sword lowered. Temptation is gold sliding across a table. Doubt is a man alone on a wall in the rain.
6. **Stay consistent:** same armor, same signature object, same places, same season as the episode list.
7. **End on the motif when it fits:** the bell, "We hold", or the gate.
